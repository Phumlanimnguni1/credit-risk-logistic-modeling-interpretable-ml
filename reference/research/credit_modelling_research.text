Features initially measured:

- applicant_id_hash            : Unique anonymized identifier for each applicant.

- age                          : Age of the applicant at the time of loan application.

- annual_income                : Reported yearly income of the applicant.

- employment_length_years      : Number of years the applicant has been employed.

- home_ownership               : Housing status (e.g. Mortgage, Rent, Own, Other).

- region                       : Geographic region category (e.g. North-Urban, North-Suburban, South-Urban, South-Suburban, East-Urban, East-Suburban, West-Urban, West-Suburban, Central-Urban, Central-Suburban).

- num_open_accounts            : Number of currently active credit accounts.

- num_delinquencies_2yr        : Number of delinquent accounts in the past 2 years.

- total_revolving_balance      : Outstanding balance on revolving credit accounts (e.g. credit cards).

- credit_utilisation_pct       : Ratio of revolving balance to total available credit (percentage).

- months_since_oldest_account  : Age of the oldest credit account in months.

- num_hard_inquiries_6mo       : Number of hard credit inquiries in the past 6 months.

- loan_amount                  : Principal amount requested for the loan.

- interest_rate                : Interest rate applied to the loan.

- loan_purpose                 : Reason for the loan (e.g. debt_consolidation, home_improvement, major_purchase, medical, education, small_business, other).

- dti_ratio                    : Debt-to-Income ratio (monthly debt ÷ monthly income).

- months_since_last_delinquency: Time since the applicant’s most recent delinquency.

- pct_accounts_current         : Percentage of accounts that are currently up to date.

- application_date             : Date when the loan application was submitted.

- application_dow              : Day of the week the application was filed.

- branch_code_id               : Identifier for the branch where the loan application was processed.

- months_at_current_address    : Number of months the applicant has lived at their current address.

- email_domain_type            : Type of email domain used (e.g. free, corporate, other).

- phone_verified               : Indicator true/false of whether the applicant’s phone number was verified.

- default_flag                 : Target variable — whether the applicant defaulted within 12 months (1 = default, 0 = no default).

- set                          : Partition indicator (e.g. train/test split for modelling).
